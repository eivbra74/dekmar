Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you order

Answers to what our customers ask most. Can't find the answer? Get in touch — we're happy to help.

Does the inspection cost anything?

No. The inspection and quote are always free and completely without obligation. We come out, look at the roof and give you an honest recommendation.

Do I get a fixed price?

Yes. After the inspection you receive a clear quote with a fixed price and an agreed timeline, so you know exactly what the job costs before we start.

Which areas do you cover?

We carry out roofing and sheet metal work throughout Oslo and Viken — for private homes, housing cooperatives and commercial buildings alike.

Do you use your own craftsmen?

Yes. We use only permanently employed professionals and have our own sheet metal workshop with a service department. You deal with one responsible contractor all the way.

Which materials do you use?

Only recognised, SINTEF-approved quality products — including Rockwool insulation, Copernit membrane and Aiwell drainage systems.

How quickly can you start?

It depends on the season and the scope. Get in touch, and we'll arrange an inspection quickly and give you a realistic start date in the quote.
